One line of at most 200 characters: the funniest honest error message an appliance could show — a microwave telling the truth about what it does to your food.

Entry (107 characters):

> BEEP. Your food is hot at the edges and frozen in the middle. This is not a malfunction. This is my design.

`main.py` writes that line to the file the sealed scorer reads (`$ENTRY_OUT`, `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt`).
